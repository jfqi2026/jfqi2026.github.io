The workshop will take place in Tokyo, Japan, from Wednesday 2nd to Friday 4th of December, 2026.

The workshop will be held at the [National Institute of Informatics (NII)](https://www.nii.ac.jp/en/about/access/) on the 19th floor, rooms 1902-1903.

**Address:** [2-1-2 Hitotsubashi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 101-8430, Japan](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=National%20Institute%20of%20Informatics%2C%202-1-2%20Hitotsubashi%2C%20Chiyoda-ku%2C%20Tokyo%20101-8430%2C%20Japan).<br>
